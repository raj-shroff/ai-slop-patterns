# Twenty-passage browser test

Date: 3 October 2026. All passages generated by the assistant for this test. Intended labels were set before running the cases. They are subjective editorial judgments, not independent human labels or known human authorship. Each passage was entered into the site textarea and submitted using Check text; results were read from the rendered DOM. The checker was not modified during this test.

## Findings

Of 12 passages intended to contain tells, 7 received at least one relevant flag and 5 received none. Case 6 was partial: summary openings were found, but the repeated underlying claim was missed. Of 8 ordinary-writing controls, 5 received no flags and 3 received contextually distracting flags. These are sample counts, not general accuracy estimates.

Cases 7–11 expose gaps in ornate language, inflated significance, repeated triads, paraphrased caveats, and reflexive balance. Cases 18–20 show that correctly matching a construction does not establish a writing defect. In particular, “Therapeutic reassurance without a need” asserts context that the literal matcher does not know.
